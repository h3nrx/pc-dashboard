**AirPlay: mirror your iPad or iPhone to this PC**
- New tab "AirPlay" under Tools. Switch it on, then on your iPad or iPhone: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → pick this PC.
- The picture opens in its own window, borderless and always in the right shape. Turning the iPad switches the window between portrait and landscape.
- The sound of the iPad plays on this PC, and the iPad's volume buttons work.
- A new device has to enter a 4-digit code once, which the app shows big. After that it connects without a code. You can switch the code off, or forget all paired devices.
- A small bar shows when you move the mouse: fullscreen (also F11 or a double-click), always on top, and disconnect.
- Built into the app: nothing else to install. The iPad and the PC have to be in the same Wi-Fi, and Windows asks once to allow the app in the firewall.
- AirPlay is off until you switch it on; only then does the PC show up in the network.
