**Safer clipper**
- The video encoder is now picked once with a quick test. The graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) is always used when it works. The heavy CPU encoder is only a last resort, and then at 30 fps.
- PCs that had slipped onto the CPU encoder are moved back to the graphics card automatically.
- If the recorder fails, it waits longer and longer before it tries again (5 s, 20 s, 60 s) instead of restarting right away.
- After 3 failures in 10 minutes, clipping turns itself off and tells you why. Switch it back on to try again.
- Saving, editing and previews run at a lower priority, so your game always comes first. No recorder process can use more than 2 GB of memory.
- Clipping stops when the drive is almost full, and a clip is only saved when there is enough space for it.

**Fixes**
- The highlight in the sidebar now stays on the current tab while the sidebar collapses or expands.
