The biggest update so far.

**New look**
- New sidebar with groups; it can shrink to icons only (button next to your name)
- Calmer design: no endless glints and drifting lights, quicker page changes
- All hotkeys in one place: Settings → Hotkeys

**Gaming**
- Crosshair overlay: shape, color, size, thickness, gap, opacity, center dot and outline, with a live preview
- Turn it on and off in game with Ctrl + Shift + X (works in borderless and windowed games)
- Ping to any server, with average, jitter and packet loss

**Sound**
- Now playing: Spotify or anything else, with cover, seek bar and controls
- Volume for every app on its own, plus mute

**Notes**
- Sticky notes that stay on top of everything: move, resize, six colors, opacity, checklists
- Click-through with Ctrl + Shift + N, so the mouse goes to the game below

**Storage**
- Sort the Downloads folder by type with one click, with undo, or automatically every 10 minutes
- Old installers can go to the Recycle Bin
